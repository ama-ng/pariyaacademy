Who We Are

A Legacy of Excellence & Faith

Founded with a clear purpose — to give every child the best start in life through quality education rooted in Islamic values.

Pariya Academy For Modern Science and Quran is a premier educational institution located in the heart of Pariya. We offer a comprehensive academic programme from Nursery through Senior Secondary, combining Nigeria's national curriculum with Islamic education.

Our school believes that intellectual excellence and moral character are inseparable. Every student who passes through our doors leaves equipped not just with certificates, but with the knowledge, values, and confidence to succeed in life.

We operate a fully digital result management system, giving parents and students transparent, secure access to academic records at any time.

Admission Process

1

Enquiry & Visit

Visit our school or call us to learn more about programmes, fees, and available spaces.

2

Collect Form

Pick up or download your admission form. Fill in all required details accurately.

3

Submit Documents

Submit birth certificate, previous school report cards, and two passport photographs.

4

Entrance Assessment

All new students from Primary 2 and above sit a brief entrance assessment.

5

Admission Confirmation

Receive your admission letter and welcome pack. Your child is now part of our family!
